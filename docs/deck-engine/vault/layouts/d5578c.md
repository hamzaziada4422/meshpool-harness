# Four-question agenda with accent numerals

When to use: Show the four questions the deck answers, in order.

Structure: Four rows, each with a large numeral on the left and a short question on the right; the first row is bold and accent to mark the current section, the rest are neutral.

Slots:
- slot 1 (number, up to 3 words): Section number 01
- slot 2 (heading, up to 8 words): First agenda question the section answers
- slot 3 (number, up to 3 words): Section number 02
- slot 4 (heading, up to 8 words): Second agenda question
- slot 5 (number, up to 3 words): Section number 03
- slot 6 (heading, up to 8 words): Third agenda question
- slot 7 (number, up to 3 words): Section number 04
- slot 8 (heading, up to 8 words): Fourth agenda question, often next steps
