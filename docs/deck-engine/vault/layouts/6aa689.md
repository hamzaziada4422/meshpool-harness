# Hypothesis tree with test boxes

When to use: Show that a decision rests on three claims, and how each one will be tested.

Structure: Dark question box on the left, three branching hypothesis boxes in the centre, each linked on the right to a test box that starts with 'Test:'.

Framework: issue tree

Slots:
- slot 1 (heading, up to 10 words): The decision question, written as a question
- slot 2 (heading, up to 10 words): A claim that must be true for the decision
- slot 3 (body, up to 24 words): Test: how we check the claim, with sample size or threshold
- slot 4 (heading, up to 10 words): Second claim that must be true
- slot 5 (body, up to 24 words): Test: how we check the second claim
- slot 6 (heading, up to 10 words): Third claim that must be true
- slot 7 (body, up to 24 words): Test: how we check the third claim
