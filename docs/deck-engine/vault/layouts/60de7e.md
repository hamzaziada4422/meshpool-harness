# Commentary beside indexed line chart

When to use: show a gap that opened up against a benchmark over time

Structure: Left column with an exhibit tag, a headline and short commentary; right side is a line chart with end-of-line labels; source line at the bottom.

Slots:
- slot 1 (tag, up to 5 words): exhibit number
- slot 2 (body, up to 30 words): two short commentary paragraphs explaining the gap
- slot 3 (chart): two lines over time, indexed to a base year
- slot 4 (label, up to 2 words): end label for our line with its last value
- slot 5 (label, up to 5 words): end label for the best competitor with its last value
