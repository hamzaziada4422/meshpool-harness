# Profit driver tree

When to use: show that a profit drop came from one driver, not from another.

Structure: Root box Profit on the left, two branches Revenue and Costs, then four or five sub-driver boxes to the right; the boxes that changed most are accent, and each box shows a name and a change.

Framework: Issue tree (driver tree)

Slots:
- slot 1 (label, up to 2 words): root driver name, one word
- slot 2 (value, up to 4 words): root change, dollar amount and percent
- slot 3 (label, up to 2 words): first branch name, one or two words
- slot 4 (value, up to 4 words): first branch change, dollar amount and percent
- slot 5 (label, up to 2 words): second branch name, one word
- slot 6 (value, up to 4 words): second branch change, dollar amount and percent
- slot 7 (label, up to 2 words): sub-driver name, one or two words
- slot 8 (value, up to 3 words): sub-driver change in percent
- slot 9 (label, up to 2 words): sub-driver name, one or two words
- slot 10 (value, up to 3 words): sub-driver change in percent
- slot 11 (label, up to 2 words): sub-driver name, one or two words
- slot 12 (value, up to 3 words): sub-driver change in percent
- slot 13 (label, up to 2 words): sub-driver name, one or two words
- slot 14 (value, up to 3 words): sub-driver change in percent
- slot 15 (label, up to 2 words): cost detail name, one word
- slot 16 (value, up to 3 words): cost detail change in percent
- slot 17 (label, up to 2 words): cost detail name, one word
- slot 18 (value, up to 3 words): cost detail change in percent
- slot 19 (caption, up to 20 words): one line giving the formula the tree follows
