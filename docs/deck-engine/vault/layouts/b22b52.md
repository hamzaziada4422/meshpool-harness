# Five-stage journey bar with sentiment line and callout

When to use: Show where customers get stuck along the path.

Structure: Title at the top, a row of six stage bars with one accent, a sentiment line below them with a dip, and a pale callout box with the pain point under the dip.

Framework: Customer journey map

Slots:
- slot 1 (label, up to 2 words): Stage 1 name
- slot 2 (label, up to 2 words): Stage 2 name
- slot 3 (label, up to 2 words): Stage 3 name
- slot 4 (label, up to 2 words): Stage 4 name, the pain-point stage
- slot 5 (label, up to 2 words): Stage 5 name
- slot 6 (label, up to 2 words): Stage 6 name
- slot 7 (chart): Sentiment score for each stage as a line, with a dip at the pain point
- slot 8 (body, up to 18 words): Pain point: what goes wrong and why, in one or two lines
