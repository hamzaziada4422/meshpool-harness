# SCR executive summary

When to use: Give the answer and its logic on one page.

Structure: Action title at the top, a one-line context line, then three labelled rows (Situation, Complication, Recommendation), each with a label on the left and a text block on the right, split by thin rules.

Framework: SCR (Situation, Complication, Recommendation)

Slots:
- slot 1 (label, up to 2 words): Row label: Situation
- slot 2 (body, up to 30 words): Situation: the finding, the evidence and what it means
- slot 3 (label, up to 2 words): Row label: Complication
- slot 4 (body, up to 30 words): Complication: what changed or what blocks progress
- slot 5 (label, up to 2 words): Row label: Recommendation
- slot 6 (body, up to 30 words): Recommendation: the action to take and its expected effect
