# Dark quote box beside two rating figures

When to use: pair a customer voice with two scores to show where the gap is

Structure: Action title at top; a dark box with a quote and attribution on the left; two stacked figures on the right, each with a short caption; source line at bottom.

Slots:
- slot 1 (body, up to 30 words): one or two sentences from a customer or patient
- slot 2 (caption, up to 8 words): who said it: role and organization
- slot 3 (value, up to 3 words): first score
- slot 4 (caption, up to 8 words): what the first score measures
- slot 5 (value, up to 3 words): second score
- slot 6 (caption, up to 12 words): what the second score measures and why it is the lowest
