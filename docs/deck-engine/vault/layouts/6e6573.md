# Recommendation banner over three columns

When to use: state one decision and the three reasons behind it on a single page.

Structure: Action title, a full-width accent banner holding the recommendation, then three equal columns each with a heading and bullet points.

Slots:
- slot 1 (heading, up to 25 words): the recommendation in one sentence, shown in the accent banner
- slot 2 (heading, up to 3 words): the word Why or a short column name
- slot 3 (body, up to 45 words): two or three short bullets with the reasons
- slot 4 (heading, up to 4 words): the word What it takes or a short column name
- slot 5 (body, up to 40 words): two or three short bullets with the cost and resources
- slot 6 (heading, up to 4 words): short heading
- slot 7 (body, up to 40 words): two or three short bullets with the expected gains
