# Situation, complication, recommendation columns

When to use: Frame the recommendation as the answer to a problem the audience already feels.

Structure: Headline on top, then three equal columns, each with a small caps label and short body text; the last column is filled accent to mark the recommendation.

Framework: SCR (situation, complication, resolution)

Slots:
- slot 1 (label, up to 2 words): Column label, one word, caps
- slot 2 (body, up to 30 words): Facts that set the scene, two to three short sentences
- slot 3 (label, up to 2 words): Column label, one word, caps
- slot 4 (body, up to 30 words): What changed or went wrong, with one number
- slot 5 (label, up to 2 words): Column label, one word, caps
- slot 6 (body, up to 30 words): The action proposed and the expected result
