# Ranked horizontal bars with takeaway sidebar

When to use: prove that one driver explains an outcome far better than others.

Structure: Action title across the top, horizontal bar chart on the left with the top bar in accent, and three bold callouts with short text stacked on the right; source line at the bottom.

Slots:
- slot 1 (chart): five ranked bars of outcome rates by driver, top bar highlighted
- slot 2 (heading, up to 5 words): short label for the first callout
- slot 3 (body, up to 22 words): one or two sentences with the two rates being compared
- slot 4 (heading, up to 5 words): short label for the second callout
- slot 5 (body, up to 22 words): one or two sentences giving the second driver's rate
- slot 6 (heading, up to 5 words): short label for the third callout
- slot 7 (body, up to 18 words): one short sentence on the weakest driver
