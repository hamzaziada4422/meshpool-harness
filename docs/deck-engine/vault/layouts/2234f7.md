# Growth-share matrix with recommendations

When to use: Show which businesses fund growth and which to invest in or exit.

Structure: 2x2 grid with market share across and market growth up. Four labelled quadrants, bubbles sized by revenue sit in them. A right column gives one recommendation per quadrant.

Framework: growth-share matrix

Slots:
- slot 1 (axis, up to 6 words): axis label
- slot 2 (axis, up to 4 words): y-axis label, market growth
- slot 3 (chart): one bubble per business unit: share, growth and revenue size
- slot 4 (body, up to 25 words): one invest, hold or exit line per quadrant, with unit names
